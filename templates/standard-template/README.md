# standard-template

Starter for a new standard at `standards/<name>/README.md`. A standard is a normative, technology-agnostic contract that skills conform to and link from their Output-contract section. Keep it prescriptive (rules, not prose) and binary-verifiable where possible. Copy the skeleton below, fill every section, and delete this paragraph.

---
